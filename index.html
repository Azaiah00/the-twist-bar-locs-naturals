Good to know

Questions, answered.

Straight answers from Tia’s Booksy page and menu.

Do you take walk-ins?

No. The Twist Bar is by appointment only, and there is no waiting area. Book your time on Booksy and arrive at your appointment time.

Where is The Twist Bar?

4135 Rivers Ave, Unit A, North Charleston, SC 29418. Use Get directions for Google Maps, or call (843) 478-3655.

What are your hours?

Tuesday and Wednesday 9 AM to 7 PM, Thursday and Friday 9 AM to 9 PM, and Saturday 7 AM to 3 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday. Booksy shows Tia's live openings.

How much is a loc retwist?

A ReTwist starts at $83 and takes about 1 hour 45 minutes. Retwist & basic style, with a 3-part shampoo and deep condition, starts at $95. A kids retwist (ages 5 to 12) is $75.

Can you save locs that are damaged?

That is what Loc Rescue is for. Tia's “I messed up. Can you save my locs?” service is a 3-month repair regimen starting at $200. Start with a $25 consultation so she can see your locs and set the plan. How Loc Rescue works.

Do you install micro locs?

Yes. A micro loc starter is $700 and takes about 12 and a half hours, and there is a $25 micro loc install consultation. Micro loc retightening on schedule is $150.

Do you offer gift cards or loyalty discounts?

Gift cards are available on Booksy. Loyal clients of 2+ years get 50% off on their birthday, and Silver (elders, 1+ year) and Gold (3+ years) members get 10% off. See the loyalty perks.
